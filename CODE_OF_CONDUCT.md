# Code of Conduct

## Expected Conduct

Be respectful and specific. Discuss the work, not the person. If your words or
actions cause harm, accept a correction.

Do not harass, threaten, insult, or discriminate against another participant.
Do not publish private information. Do not use sexualized language or unwanted
attention.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces that represent this project.

## Reporting

Contact the repository maintainer for a conduct concern. Do not put private
details in a public issue.

If no private maintainer channel is available, use
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) for abuse or
an urgent safety concern.

## Enforcement

When possible, the maintainer protects private details during a review. A
response can include a correction request, a temporary restriction, or a
permanent ban from project spaces.

GitHub can also remove content or restrict accounts under its own policies.
